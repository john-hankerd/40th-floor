Event Hub

Volunteers, donations, and sponsors in a couple of taps.

Event Hub helps local groups list events and get volunteers, donations, and sponsors in a couple of taps. The public needs no account to browse, sign up, give, or get alerts. It is in early access. Payments, texts, and emails are simulated for now: no money moves, and no texts or emails go out. Web Push can send.

Open Event Hub In testing · Early access · Your first event gets Event Pass tools free

What you get

📅

Event page

An event page with the schedule, an FAQ, and a map. Activity tabs with volunteer shifts showing spots left.

✍️

Signup with no account

The public needs no account to browse, sign up, give, or get alerts. Volunteer signup asks for just a name, phone, and email.

💬

Reminders and check-in

Email reminders on every plan (emails are simulated in early access). With Event Pass: a reminder text with one-tap drop or swap (texts are simulated in early access), QR check-in, and printable student service hours. Age limits and parent or guardian signup.

📣

Broadcasts

Organizer broadcasts to volunteers, sponsors, and alert subscribers. In early access, text and email broadcasts are logged, not sent. Web Push can send.

👥

Group pages

Group pages: people can follow your group and get your news between events by email, text, or push. Included in Annual; Free groups get a page, followers, and 4 email updates a month. Texts and emails are simulated in early access. Web Push can send.

💛

Donations

Donations with an optional ‘cover fees’ box that starts unchecked. Event Hub keeps a 2% platform fee on each gift, with no tips, ever. The card processor charges 2.9% + 30¢. On a $50 gift the group receives $47.25, or the full $50 if the donor covers the fees. Test checkout for now: no money moves.

🤝

Wish list and sponsors

A wish list. Sponsor levels with a benefits checklist and an after-event sponsor report.

✅

Committee

Committee tasks and a timeline (Event Pass). Copy last year's event (Annual).

📍

Free local events hub

A free local events hub. The test showcase is built around the Owosso Glow in Owosso, MI. Only the Glow 5K date (Fri, Nov 27, 2026, 5:30 PM) is real; the rest is sample data.

Pricing

🆓

Free — $0

Hub listing, one signup with up to 25 slots, email reminders, a donation page, a group page with followers (email updates, 4 a month), and no ads. Donations on every plan carry the 2% platform fee plus card processing.

🎟️

Event Pass — $79 per event

Covers about 90 days around your event date. Adds unlimited slots, text reminders (500-message fair-use cap), QR check-in and student hours, sponsors, a wish list, and committee tools.

📆

Annual — $299/yr

Unlimited events. Everything in Event Pass, plus group updates by text, email, and push between events, and copy last year's event. Bought an Event Pass in the last 60 days? It credits $79, so Annual is $220.

🎁

Your first event is free

Your first event gets Event Pass tools at no charge, one per organizer email. It's offered instead of a time-limited trial. Event Hub is in early access, so payments, texts, and emails are simulated for now.
