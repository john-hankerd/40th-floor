Event Hub

Volunteers, donations, and sponsors in a couple of taps.

Event Hub helps local groups list events and get volunteers, donations, and sponsors in a couple of taps. The public needs no account to browse, sign up, give, or get alerts. It is in testing and early access. Payments and texts aren't live yet.

Open Event Hub In testing · Early access · The first event is free

What you get

📅

Event page

An event page with the schedule, an FAQ, and a map. Activity tabs with volunteer shifts showing spots left.

✍️

Signup with no account

The public needs no account to browse, sign up, give, or get alerts. Volunteer signup asks for just a name, phone, and email.

💬

One reminder text

One reminder text, with one-tap drop or swap. QR check-in. Printable student service hours. Age limits and parent signup.

📣

Broadcasts

Organizer broadcasts to volunteers, sponsors, and alert subscribers.

💛

Donations

Donations with an optional ‘cover fees’ box. ‘2% platform fee, no tips, ever’ plus standard card processing (2.9% + 30c).

🤝

Wish list and sponsors

A wish list. Sponsor levels with a benefits checklist and an after-event sponsor report.

✅

Committee

Committee tasks and a timeline. Copy last year's event.

📍

Free local events hub

A free local events hub. The showcase is the Owosso Glow in Owosso, MI.

Pricing

🆓
