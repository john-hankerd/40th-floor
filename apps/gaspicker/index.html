GasPicker

Not the cheapest gas near you. The cheapest stop for your actual trip.

Tell GasPicker where you're headed and how many miles you've got left. It maps your route, figures out how far you can really go, and tells you the one station worth pulling off for — weighing price, the detour to get there, and how much you'll actually need to fill up. No account, no app store, no credit card.

Open GasPicker Free · In testing

What you get

🧭

Built for the trip, not the moment

Most gas apps show what's cheap nearby right now. GasPicker looks at your whole route and picks the stop that actually saves you money once the detour and fill-up size are factored in.

⛽

Never guesses your range

Just enter miles to empty (right off your dashboard) and your tank size — GasPicker only recommends stations you can actually reach, with a safety cushion built in.

🤝

Gets smarter as more people use it

Prices are reported by real drivers. The more people who use GasPicker near a station, the more current and accurate its price becomes.
