The problem

Your rating never tells the whole story.

Happy campers rarely leave a review. Unhappy ones almost always do. And a Google search won't tell you what to actually fix.

Happy → quiet

Your best guests leave without a word, so your rating sits lower than the work you do.

Unhappy → loud

One bad night becomes a public 1-star before you ever hear about it.

Google → vague

Search gives you listicles and generic advice — never what your campers actually said.

What you get

Every review, turned into something you can act on.

The number, not a hunch

We count every opinion, so you see the real story: "your #1 complaint is bathhouse cleanliness — here's how often it comes up."

Fix these first

A short, ranked list of what to fix — with the specific thing top parks actually did about it. No fluff, no "improve this area."

The receipts

Every number links to a real quote from a real guest, word-for-word. So you can trust it — and act on it with confidence.

You vs. the field
