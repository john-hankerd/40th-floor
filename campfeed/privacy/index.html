Privacy Policy

This policy explains what CampFeed collects, how we use it, and the choices you have — with specific detail about Google review data.

Last updated: August 24, 2026

CampFeed is a campground review-analysis service operated by John Hankerd (a 40th Floor project), Owosso, Michigan. It helps campground owners understand what guests say about their park and how they compare to the industry. This policy applies to the CampFeed website and app. Questions? Email john.hankerd@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details you give us — your name, email, and campground name — when you sign up or request a report.
  • Review and feedback data from sources you connect or upload (for example, your Google Business Profile reviews, your All5Starz feedback, or a review export you upload), and from public sources we analyze to build industry benchmarks.
  • Basic usage data needed to run the service (such as log data).
What we do not store: we do not keep the personal identity of the people who wrote reviews — no reviewer names, emails, or profile links. We keep only the star rating, the review text, and the date, so we can count and quote opinions.

Google user data

If you choose to connect your Google Business Profile, CampFeed accesses your business's own reviews — the star rating, the review text, and the date — through the Google Business Profile API, and only after you authorize it.

  • We use this data solely to provide the service to you: to generate your CampFeed report and to contribute to anonymized, aggregated industry benchmarks. We never publish your reviews wholesale or attach a competitor's name to anything.
  • We do not sell your Google data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized machine-learning models.
  • CampFeed's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • You can disconnect at any time and ask us to delete the data we pulled from your listing (see “Your choices and deletion”).

How we use data

We use the data above to generate your report, build anonymized industry benchmarks, operate and improve the service, and contact you about your account. That's it.

How we share data

We do not sell your data. On any public part of the service we show only our own analysis — counts, themes, and at most a short attributed quote with a link back — never a full corpus of reviews and never a named competitor. We use a small number of service providers (such as our database host and payment processor) to run CampFeed; they may process data only to provide their service to us. We may disclose data if required by law.

Storage and security

Data is stored on reputable cloud infrastructure. Access credentials you grant (like a Google authorization token) are stored securely and never shared. We take reasonable measures to protect data, though no system is perfectly secure.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. You can request deletion of your data — including anything pulled from a connected Google account — at any time by emailing john.hankerd@gmail.com, and we will delete it within a reasonable period.

Your choices and deletion

  • Disconnect Google: revoke CampFeed's access anytime in your Google Account permissions, and email us to remove the associated data.
  • Access or delete: email us to see, correct, or delete the data we hold about you.

Children

CampFeed is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We'll change the “Last updated” date above and, for significant changes, make reasonable efforts to notify you.
